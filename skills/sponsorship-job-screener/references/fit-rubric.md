# Resume-to-JD fit rubric

## Principle

Score evidence for the work described in the full JD. Use the title only as a modest role-family prior. Reward direct, recent, quantified evidence; do not reward keyword repetition or infer skills from job titles.

## Score out of 100

- Role-family prior: 10
- Must-have qualifications and required experience: 35
- Core responsibilities and business outcomes: 25
- Tools, methods, and domain knowledge: 20
- Seniority, ownership, and stakeholder scope: 10

For each requirement label candidate evidence `Direct`, `Adjacent`, `Missing`, or `Contradicted`. Link every `Direct` or `Adjacent` judgment to a factual resume bullet or profile entry.

Cap fit at 69 when a genuine hard must-have is missing. Do not penalize a missing nice-to-have as a requirement. Do not award exact-tool credit for a merely adjacent tool when production experience in a named platform is mandatory.

## Batch consistency

Build one capability inventory from the current private candidate evidence and apply the same rubric throughout the run. Rebuild it only when the evidence or target functions change. Explain the two strongest matches and no more than two material gaps for qualifying roles.
